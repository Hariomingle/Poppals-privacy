COPPA & GDPR-K Compliant

PopPals Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30-10-2026
Developer / Publisher: BrightBubble Studios (Hariom Ingle)

Summary for Parents & Guardians: PopPals is a colorful educational bubble-popping game designed for young children (ages 2–7). We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, photos, voice recordings, or location data from you or your child. All ads shown are strictly non-personalized, G-rated, child-directed ads via Google AdMob with advertising ID tracking disabled.

1. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data Collection)

BrightBubble Studios does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) from users of PopPals. Specifically:

  • No Accounts or Registration: PopPals never asks for names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, or login credentials.
  • No Location Data: PopPals never requests or accesses coarse or fine device location data.
  • No Camera, Microphone, or Photos Access: PopPals never accesses your device camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • No Advertising ID Tracking: The Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions are explicitly stripped from the app manifest.

2. Child-Safe Non-Personalized Advertising (Google AdMob)

PopPals displays contextual, non-personalized Banner and Interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a Google Play Families self-certified ad SDK). To protect children's privacy:

  • Every ad request is configured with Child-Directed Treatment (COPPA) and Under Age of Consent (GDPR-K) enabled.
  • All advertisements are restricted to Maximum Ad Content Rating: G (General Audiences).
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: Ads are contextual only and never use behavioral profiling or advertising identifiers.
  • Offline Friendly: When the device is offline, PopPals functions 100% normally without ads.

3. Local On-Device Storage Only

Game progress—such as unlocked sticker book items, high scores, sound/voice preferences, and in-app unlock status—is saved strictly locally on your device using standard platform preferences (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is permanently erased if you uninstall the application.

4. Parent-Gated In-App Purchases

PopPals offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (PopPals Full Unlock) processed entirely through the official platform store (Google Play Billing or Apple App Store StoreKit).

  • Access to the purchase button is protected behind a Parent Gate (an arithmetic challenge and a 2-second hold confirmation designed for adults).
  • All payment processing is handled directly by Google Play or Apple. PopPals never receives or has access to your billing name, credit card details, or financial information.

5. Compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K & Store Families Policies

PopPals is built from the ground up to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation provisions for children (GDPR-K), the Google Play Families Policy, and the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines:

  • No third-party social media links or un-gated external web links exist inside the app.
  • Spoken voice prompts are generated strictly on-device using your operating system's built-in Text-to-Speech engine.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version at this URL and update the "Effective Date" at the top of the page.
